The group works on **process systems engineering (PSE)** — building process models and using optimization to design chemical processes that are cleaner, cheaper, and more energy-efficient than the conventional route.

The directions below are organised by **what we design**. The methods we use cut across all of them and are described separately on [Methods & capabilities](/research/methods/).

New directions open as projects and funding arrive; **natural-product and essential-oil processing**, **hydrocarbon and coal processing** and **palm oil processing** are the two we intend to open next. They are not listed as directions here until there is work behind them.

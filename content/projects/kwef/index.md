**Funder:** Kurita Water and Environment Foundation (KWEF) — Overseas Research Grant
**Grant ID:** 26Pid194 · **Term:** October 2026 – September 2027
**Host:** Institut Teknologi Kalimantan

A hybrid, physics-informed machine-learning framework for modelling **palm oil
mill effluent (POME) treatment**. The project builds a mechanistic core in DWSIM
(anaerobic digestion via ADM1, with simplified aerobic polishing) and couples it
with machine learning to predict effluent quality from low-cost measurements.

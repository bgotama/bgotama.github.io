The Gotama Research Group welcomes motivated **undergraduate and thesis
students** at ITK, as well as collaborators interested in process modelling,
simulation, and optimization for low-carbon fuels and wastewater treatment.

### What we look for
- Curiosity and persistence with modelling and data
- Interest in Aspen Plus / DWSIM, Python, or optimization
- Willingness to work openly and reproducibly

### How to apply
Email **you@itk.ac.id** with:

1. A short paragraph on why you want to join and what interests you
2. Your CV or transcript
3. (Optional) any prior project, code, or writing you're proud of

I read every email personally. Applications that show you've read this page and
our research pages get my full attention.

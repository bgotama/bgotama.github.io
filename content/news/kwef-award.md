The Gotama Research Group has been awarded the **Kurita Water and Environment
Foundation (KWEF) Overseas Research Grant 2026** to develop a hybrid,
physics-informed modelling framework for palm oil mill effluent (POME)
treatment. The project runs October 2026 – September 2027 at ITK.

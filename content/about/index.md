I am **Bangkit Gotama**, a lecturer at Chemical Engineering Department (che.itk.ac.id) Institut Teknologi Kalimantan (ITK) and
the lead of the Gotama Research Group. I hold a Ph.D. in Chemical Engineering
from the National Taiwan University of Science and Technology (NTUST).

My research sits in **Process Systems Engineering (PSE)**: I build process
models and use optimization to design cleaner, more efficient chemical
processes. My doctoral work focused on hydroprocessing pathways that convert
**lipid feedstock** into renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel (SAF),
coupling Aspen Plus simulations with multi-objective optimization.

### Interests
- Multi-objective process optimization 
- Drop-in biofuels production
- Waste feedstock valorization
- Process simulation automation (Aspen Plus, Matlab, Python, DWSIM)
